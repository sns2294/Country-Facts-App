Syria

سوريا

Syria is a transitional presidential republic, located in Western Asia. Islam is the predominant religion. Its capital is Damascus. About 25.6 million people live there (2025).

The Golan Heights, captured from Syria in 1967 and annexed by Israel in 1981, is internationally recognized as Syrian territory; a few countries, including the United States, recognize it as part of Israel.

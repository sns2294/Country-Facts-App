Political map of East Asia

6 countries, each colored differently from its neighbors. Dots mark capital cities.

Tap the map to open it full size, then pinch to zoom.Click the map to open it full screen, then scroll to zoom and drag to move.

Borders: Natural Earth (public domain). Capital cities: Natural Earth populated places. Drawn in the Lambert azimuthal equal-area projection. Taiwan is shown as its own entry, as on the site; Hong Kong and Macao take China's color. Areas claimed by more than one country are given to none and hatched: Kashmir (including Aksai Chin), the Southern Kuril Islands (administered by Russia, claimed by Japan), the Senkaku/Diaoyu Islands (administered by Japan, claimed by China and Taiwan) and Dokdo/Takeshima (administered by South Korea, claimed by Japan); the last two are marks, not to scale. The South China Sea islands lie outside this map.

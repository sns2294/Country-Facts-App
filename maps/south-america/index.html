Political map of South America

12 countries, each colored differently from its neighbors. Dots mark capital cities. French Guiana, the Falkland Islands and South Georgia are in their parent country's color, with the parent in parentheses; an inset shows the Galápagos Islands.

Borders: Natural Earth (public domain). Capital cities: Natural Earth populated places. Drawn in the Lambert azimuthal equal-area projection. French Guiana is an overseas department of France; the Falkland Islands and South Georgia and the South Sandwich Islands are British Overseas Territories, and Argentina claims them. Venezuela claims Guyana's Essequibo region, about two-thirds of Guyana; the map shows the internationally recognized border, and the dispute is pending before the International Court of Justice. Sucre is Bolivia's constitutional capital; La Paz is its seat of government.

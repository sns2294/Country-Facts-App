Kashmir is disputed: India and Pakistan each administer part of it and claim all of it, and China administers Aksai Chin and the Shaksgam Valley, which India claims. The map assigns none of it to a country; it is hatched, with dashed lines where the administered parts meet. The Siachen Glacier is shown the same way.

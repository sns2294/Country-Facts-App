Political map of Southeast Asia

11 countries, each colored differently from its neighbors. Dots mark capital cities; the smallest states are labeled with a pointer line.

Tap the map to open it full size, then pinch to zoom.Click the map to open it full screen, then scroll to zoom and drag to move.

Borders: Natural Earth (public domain). Capital cities: Natural Earth populated places. Drawn in the Lambert azimuthal equal-area projection. The Paracel Islands (administered by China, claimed by Vietnam and Taiwan), the Spratly Islands (claimed in whole or part by China, Taiwan, Vietnam, the Philippines, Malaysia and Brunei) and Scarborough Shoal (claimed by China, the Philippines and Taiwan) are given to none and shown hatched: the ovals mark the area the scattered islands lie in, not claimed waters. Sabah is shown as part of Malaysia; the Philippines also claims it.
